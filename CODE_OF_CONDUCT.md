# Code of conduct

Headroom follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or publishing anyone's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer,
[zaryabmustafa](https://github.com/zaryabmustafa), through their GitHub profile, or use
GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who
break these rules, depending on how serious it is.
